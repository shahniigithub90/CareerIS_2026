Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Launch Your Career

Built exclusively for JUW — manage internships, certificates, alumni connections, and more from one powerful platform.

Internship Portal
Browse and apply for real internships posted by JUW alumni. Filter by type, location, and more. Track your application status in real time.
Certificate Verification
Upload your certifications for admin verification. Share a verified link with employers proving your credentials are authentic and endorsed by JUW.
Alumni Network
Connect with JUW graduates working at top companies. Browse alumni by batch year, company, or domain. Reach out via LinkedIn or email directly.
Resume Builder
Auto-generate a professional PDF resume using your profile, certificates, and work experience. Download it ready for applications.
Announcements
Stay updated with university notices, career events, deadlines, and important messages from admin and alumni in one feed.
Secure & Role-Based
JWT-secured access with three separate portals for Students, Alumni, and Admins. Everyone sees only what they need — nothing more.
