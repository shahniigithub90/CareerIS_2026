Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CareerIS

What is CareerIS?
CareerIS is a Career Information System designed as a Final Year Project. It integrates three portals: Alumni Portal, Internship Portal, and Certification Portal to connect students, alumni, and industry opportunities.
Who can use CareerIS?
CareerIS has three user roles: Students (current university students), Alumni (graduated students), and Administrators (university staff). Each role has specific permissions and access levels.
How do I get access to CareerIS?
Access is role-based. Students can register with university credentials. Alumni status requires graduation verification and admin approval. Admin access is granted to university staff only.
Is CareerIS free to use?
Yes, CareerIS is completely free for students, alumni, and university staff. It's developed as an academic project to enhance university career services.
How are certificates verified?
Students upload certificates which are then verified by administrators. The admin team checks certificate authenticity before marking them as verified in the system.
Can I contribute to CareerIS development?
Currently, CareerIS is a Final Year Project. However, we welcome feedback and suggestions. For collaboration opportunities, please contact us through the form above.
