Platform Features

Explore the comprehensive features of our integrated career management platform

Three Integrated Portals

Each portal serves specific needs while working together seamlessly

Alumni Portal

The Alumni Portal creates a vibrant network where graduated students can reconnect with their alma mater and support current students through mentorship and opportunities.

Profile Management

Create and update professional profiles

Directory Search

Find and connect with other alumni

Job Posting

Post opportunities for students

Batch Reunions

Organize batch gatherings

Internship Portal

The Internship Portal streamlines the process of finding, applying for, and managing internship opportunities, connecting students directly with companies and alumni recruiters.

Listings & Search

Find internships by role, company, location

Resume Upload

Upload and manage CVs/resumes

Quick Applications

One-click apply with saved profiles

Save Listings

Bookmark favorite opportunities

Certification Portal

The Certification Portal allows students to build a verifiable digital portfolio of their skills and certifications, making it easier to showcase qualifications to potential employers.

Certificate Upload

Upload PDF/image certificates

Verification System

Admin verification for authenticity

Certificate Download

Download certificates anytime

Certificate Management

Organize and manage certificates
