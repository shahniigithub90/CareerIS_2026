What is CareerIS?

CareerIS (Career Information System) is a web-based platform designed to support career development within a university environment. The system connects students, alumni, and administrators through a centralized digital interface.

Students can create profiles, explore internship opportunities, and upload or track their certifications. Alumni can maintain professional profiles, post internships, and guide students through mentorship. Administrators manage user accounts, approve certifications, and monitor system activities.

Our Mission & Vision

Driving career development in the digital age

Our Mission

To create a seamless digital bridge between university students and professional opportunities by leveraging alumni networks and industry partnerships, thereby enhancing employability and career readiness for every student.

Our Vision

To become the standard career management platform for universities, transforming how educational institutions prepare students for successful professional lives through technology-enabled connections and alumni mentorship.

Project Timeline

The development journey of CareerIS

Phase 1 — Sep 2025
Project Inception

Identified the problem of fragmented career services at JUW. Defined scope for Alumni, Internship, and Certification portals.

Phase 2 — Oct 2025
Requirements & Design

Gathered requirements from stakeholders. Created wireframes, database schema, and system architecture diagrams.

Phase 3 — Nov–Dec 2025
Development

Built the full-stack application using Node.js, Express, MongoDB, and Bootstrap. Implemented JWT authentication and role-based access.

Phase 4 — Jan–Feb 2026
Testing & Refinement

Conducted thorough testing across all portals. Refined UI/UX based on user feedback and resolved bugs.

Phase 5 — Mar 2026
Final Presentation

Final year project submission and presentation to the evaluation committee at Jinnah University for Women.
